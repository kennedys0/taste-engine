# Style pack: Y2K Futurism

**ID:** `y2k-futurism`  
**Signature:** "The Millennium Future" — late-1990s and early-2000s cyber-optimism, liquid-chrome materiality, molded translucent colored plastics, controlled holographic spectral reflections, sculptural 3D-inspired vector geometry, expressive wide futuristic typography, and tactile hardware interfaces.

> This is a **style pack, not a mandatory default**. Load it only after explicit selection or confirmed applicable project style. Project instructions can override individual choices. Preserve accessibility, utility, and brand constraints. A generic dark SaaS dashboard or purple Vaporwave gradient page **does not qualify as Y2K Futurism**.

## Good fit / trade-offs

- **Works well for:** Creative studios, electronic music & audio synthesis platforms, cutting-edge fashion and lifestyle labels, experimental portfolios, speculative technology products, gaming ecosystems, and forward-looking digital culture apps.
- **Use with care for:** Traditional corporate banking portals, clinical medical records, or conservative legal documentation where synthetic metallic materiality may distract.
- **Creative Direction — The Millennium Future:** Imagine a digital future designed in the year 2000: optimistic, experimental, synthetic, metallic, tactile, and visually expressive. Recall reflective consumer electronics, liquid-metal graphics, transparent colored polymer shells, futuristic album artwork, early CGI aesthetics, chrome hardware interfaces, and holographic media packaging.
- **Reference directions (3 Native Expressions):**
  - *Chrome (Default):* Lighter, reflective, silver-dominant metallic environment (`#F1F3F6` / `#E5E9F1` / `#C5CBD3`), multi-band liquid-chrome surfaces with crisp horizon reflections, sculptural liquid-metal orb & orbital ring centerpiece, high-gloss beveled hardware controls, and restrained ice-blue (`#90DCFF`) or electric-blue (`#5276FF`) accents.
  - *Holographic:* Pearlescent and iridescent surfaces (`#F3EAFD`), controlled spectral color transitions (`#90DCFF` → `#5276FF` → `#A78AFF` → `#F2A9DC`), molded translucent colored plastic panels, refractive prism centerpiece, and experimental futuristic editorial framing.
  - *Retro Tech:* Late-1990s / early-2000s hardware-inspired digital interface featuring molded metallic chassis (`#252833` / `#181821`), industrial chrome seams, recessed LCD/vector telemetry readouts, physical transport buttons, and a segmented orbital gyroscope centerpiece.

## Y2K Futurism material fidelity gate — mandatory

**Before calling a Y2K Futurism page complete, the opening viewport must visibly read as an authentic millennium metallic and synthetic composition.** If a user chose this style, a conventional dark-themed SaaS landing page with centered text, 4 generic stat cards, and thin grey outlines is a **FAIL**, even if CSS classes contain `y2k`.

A successful Y2K Futurism implementation demonstrates **all** of the following:

1. **Four-Part Synthetic Material System:**
   - **Liquid Chrome:** Multi-band light and shadow horizon sequences (`linear-gradient(180deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #E5E9F1 24%, #A4ADBF 48%, #424B60 52%, #C5CBD3 78%, #FFFFFF 100%)`) with sharp specular transitions and cool environmental reflections rather than flat grey fills.
   - **Iridescent Spectral Material:** Controlled spectral transitions (`#90DCFF`, `#5276FF`, `#A78AFF`, `#F2A9DC`, `#F3EAFD`, `#8FCFE9`, `#8865DD`, `#E6ABD8`) used for focal highlights, optical rings, and active accents without muddying text legibility.
   - **Translucent Colored Plastic:** Molded semi-transparent polymer surfaces inspired by year-2000 translucent consumer electronics, featuring distinct top-edge specular highlights, subtle internal illumination, and crisp molded borders.
   - **Synthetic Metallic Hardware Panels:** Beveled metallic containers with molded corners, recessed physical inset wells, subtle seam lines, and directional lighting instead of flat dark dashboard cards.
2. **Asymmetric Hero with Recognizable Visual Centerpiece:** The hero must reject plain centered SaaS headers and generic four-column stat rows in favor of an asymmetric 3-region composition anchored by a bespoke Y2K centerpiece (reflective liquid-metal orb, sculptural chrome orbital rings, or iridescent synthetic geometry).
3. **Three-Tier Typography Hierarchy:** Expressive wide/extended futuristic display typography (with liquid-chrome fills, outline-and-fill lockups, or slanted techno accents) paired with geometric technical interface labels and clean, effortless body copy.
4. **Deep Variant Differentiation:** Switching between `Chrome`, `Holographic`, and `Retro Tech` must transform material treatment, typography accents, decorative geometry, panel chassis, primary visual object, and control surfaces — not merely swap an accent hex code.
5. **Anti-Clash Text Contrast (WCAG AA 4.5:1):** Deep graphite ink (`#181821` or `#1D2331`) on bright chrome, pearl, and silver surfaces; crisp chrome-white (`#F1F3F6` or `#FFFFFF`) on deep graphite hardware bays.
6. **Zero Self-Congratulatory Badge Slop:** Never clutter the hero with generic engineering boast pills (*"WCAG AA 4.5:1"*, *"Zero AI-Slop"*, *"120ms Motion"*, *"New 10th Style"*). Demonstrate quality through visual execution.

**First-viewport test:** In a desktop screenshot, identify the multi-band liquid chrome reflections, the sculptural Y2K centerpiece, the asymmetric futuristic display typography, and the molded hardware controls **without reading the page title**. If it could be mistaken for Cyberpunk, generic Web3, or a conventional dark SaaS template, rebuild the materiality and composition.

## Explicit Y2K Futurism material exception — chrome and iridescence are allowed

**Y2K Futurism-specific override:** The universal anti-slop caution against *metallic gradients, iridescent highlights, translucent plastic layers, or sculptural SVG centerpieces* MUST NOT be interpreted as a ban on multi-stop horizon chrome gradients, holographic light fields, molded plastic cards, or bespoke Y2K vector sculptures here. These are **material-enabling properties**, essential to communicating the authentic millennium digital vision.

- **Allowed material treatments:** Multi-stop liquid-chrome horizon gradients, SVG `feSpecularLighting` / metallic gradient sculptures, iridescent linear and conic accent borders, molded translucent colored plastic panels, chamfered/beveled hardware corners, and recessed LCD telemetry wells.
- **Harmonious color rules:** Anchor palettes in metallic silver, mirror chrome, pearl, deep graphite, ice blue, electric blue, orchid violet, and cyber pink. Avoid harsh unharmonized neon cyberpunk clashes.
- **Anti-slop boundary:** Every interactive control must be functional and accessible. Do not paste unstyled stock photos, fake spinning low-effort GIFs, or self-congratulatory quality badges in the hero, and never apply chrome or rainbow gradients to multi-line body paragraphs.
- **Anti-slop interpretation:** Evaluate Y2K details by **material authenticity, lighting coherence, compositional boldness, and functional legibility**, not by counting gradient stops. Flattening chrome surfaces into plain dark cards is a **severe design regression**.

## Typography and typewriting

Y2K Futurism requires a strict **Three-Tier Typography System** that contributes directly to the millennium visual identity:

- **1. Display Typography (`--font-y2k-display`):**
  - **Typefaces:** Extended futuristic display faces (`Orbitron`, `Syne`, wide geometric techno sans).
  - **Treatments:** Strong scale contrast (`clamp(2.5rem, 5.5vw, 4.75rem)`), wide tracking (`0.04em` to `0.1em`), uppercase lockups, liquid-chrome metallic text fills (`background-clip: text`), hollow outline-and-fill combinations (`-webkit-text-stroke`), and controlled slanted/italic techno emphasis (`transform: skewX(-8deg)`).
  - **Restraint:** Reserve chrome and outline treatments strictly for hero headlines, section numerals, and short focal lockups.
- **2. Interface Typography (`--font-y2k-ui`):**
  - **Typefaces:** Clean geometric technical sans (`Space Grotesk`) paired with crisp technical mono (`JetBrains Mono`) for hardware labels, mode selectors, coordinates, and tabular readouts (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
  - **Treatments:** Uppercase micro-labels (`11px–13px`, weight `700`, tracking `0.08em`) that read like consumer-electronics or synth-hardware silkscreen legends.
- **3. Body Typography (`--font-y2k-body`):**
  - **Typefaces:** Effortlessly readable contemporary sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, system sans) at `15px–17px` desktop (`14px–15px` mobile) with `1.6` line-height and `60ch–68ch` measure. Never apply metallic gradients or wide tracking to body copy.

### Light Mode (Pearl Silver & Cool Metallic Environment)
- **Canvas / Background:** Pearl silver & cool mist (`#EBF0F7` to `#DDE4F0`) with subtle metallic grid reflection and iridescent horizon glow.
- **Surface Panels:** Brushed aluminum & mirror chrome (`linear-gradient(165deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #E5E9F1 55%, #D5DCE8 100%)`).
- **Translucent Plastic Shell:** Ice-blue or orchid tinted acrylic (`rgba(243, 234, 253, 0.72)`) with `1px solid #FFFFFF` top specular rim.
- **Reading Ink:** High-contrast deep chrome-graphite (`#181821` and `#1D2331`) with secondary slate (`#424B60`).

### Dark Mode (Deep Graphite & Liquid Silver Reflections)
- **Canvas / Background:** Deep space metallic chassis (`#12151E` to `#181821`) with sculpted horizon chrome lighting and controlled spectral aura.
- **Surface Panels:** Anodized gunmetal & liquid chrome (`linear-gradient(160deg, #2A3042 0%, #1D2331 52%, #151924 100%)`).
- **Translucent Plastic Shell:** Smoked cyber-blue or orchid polymer (`rgba(42, 48, 66, 0.72)`) with top specular highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`).
- **Reading Ink:** Crisp mirror-white (`#FFFFFF` / `#F1F3F6`) with metallic silver secondary copy (`#C5CBD3`).

### Theme Coupling & Anti-Collision Rule
Both Light Mode and Dark Mode must feel intentionally designed around reflective materiality — never a mechanical color inversion. When switching modes, text, bevels, and specular highlights flip in synchronized pairs maintaining ≥ 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast.

## Layout and geometry

- **Asymmetric Editorial & Hardware Framing:** Break away from monotonous centered headings and uniform 3-column or 4-column card grids. Combine asymmetric split-screen hero layouts, full-bleed metallic specification strips, interactive material laboratories, and modular studio decks.
- **Balanced Split-Column Card Stacks & Equal-Height Grids (`.taste-y2k-balanced-stack`):** Whenever a 2-column Studio bay or Component Gallery places a single tall card in Column A alongside 2–3 stacked cards in Column B, the parent grid MUST declare `align-items: stretch;`, the stacked column wrapper MUST declare `display: flex; flex-direction: column; height: 100%; justify-content: space-between;`, and each stacked child card MUST declare `flex: 1 1 0%; display: flex; flex-direction: column; justify-content: space-between;` so top and bottom edges of both columns align flush with zero empty void underneath. Never wrap 4 controls/checkboxes as `3 + 1` orphans (use a `2×2` grid) or leave dangling empty grid cells across desktop (`3×2`) and tablet (`2×3`) breakpoints.
- **Molded & Chamfered Silhouettes:** Mix sculpted capsule curves (`border-radius: 9999px` on liquid-chrome pills and orbital badges) with precision chamfered hardware corners (`clip-path` 8px–12px bevels or molded `10px–14px` chassis radii with inner recessed wells).
- **Strict 1:1 Circle Geometry (Anti-Oval):** Every circular orb, LED indicator, rotary dial, and icon button (`border-radius: 50%`) must enforce `aspect-ratio: 1 / 1; flex-shrink: 0;`.
- **Responsive Baseline:** Adhere to the [responsive baseline](../references/responsive-baseline.md) from `320px` mobile up to `1440px+` desktop without horizontal overflow.

## Component anatomy and interaction

- **Vector Iconography & Sculptural Motifs:** Emojis are strictly banned. Use precision vector icons (`1.5px–2px` stroke) and bespoke SVG chrome/orbital geometry.
- **Primary Liquid-Chrome Button:** Multi-stop horizon reflection fill (`#FFFFFF` → `#E5E9F1` → `#A4ADBF` → `#424B60` → `#E5E9F1`), deep graphite label (`#181821`), crisp white specular border, upward hover glint, and physical `1.5px` depression on `:active`.
- **Holographic Spectral Button:** Pearlescent iridescent gradient (`#90DCFF` → `#A78AFF` → `#F2A9DC`) with high-contrast dark ink (`#181821`) and internal top highlight.
- **Secondary Molded Hardware Button:** Brushed metallic chassis button with status LED pip and beveled edge seam.
- **Recessed Hardware Inputs & Controls:** Sunken diagnostic wells (`inset 0 2px 5px rgba(0,0,0,0.65)`), custom metallic select menus, tactile toggle switches with glowing ice-blue/orchid state pips, and clear validation states.
- **Interactive Material & Variant Controls:** Allow users to inspect materials and switch between `Chrome`, `Holographic`, and `Retro Tech` with immediate visual transformation across surfaces, centerpiece geometry, and typography accents.

## Motion

Y2K Futurism motion is synthetic, responsive, and disciplined:
- **Tactile Control Depression:** `110ms–140ms` physical button travel (`transform: translateY(1.5px)`) with specular shadow shift.
- **Specular Reflection Sweep:** Controlled highlight shift across liquid-chrome buttons and orbital rings on hover or light-angle adjustment.
- **Centerpiece Orbital Geometry:** Subtle, smooth SVG ring/satellite rotation or interactive perspective response that pauses or disables immediately under reduced motion.
- **Reduced Motion:** Under `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`, disable all continuous orbital animations and physical transforms while preserving instant state feedback.

## Hero composition (full first viewport)

**When a public-facing portfolio/landing/homepage has a hero:** apply the [Full-screen Hero Contract](../references/hero-layout.md) using a one-visible-screen header+hero composition with an unmistakable **Asymmetric 3-Region Millennium Composition**. Base height on `min-height: 100svh` with `100vh` fallback (`min-height: calc(100svh - 60px)` below the sticky header in normal flow); let the hero expand naturally for accessibility and short mobile screens. Do not apply this to private dashboards or other task screens; direct user overrides win.
- **Region A — Brand & Expressive Display Headline:** Mixed-scale futuristic typography combining a technical millennium index tag, liquid-chrome display lettering, hollow outline-and-fill lockup, and slanted techno emphasis over a concise, high-contrast thesis paragraph.
- **Region B — Primary Visual Object (Y2K Centerpiece):** One cohesive, unforgettable SVG/CSS centerpiece — a **Reflective Liquid-Metal Orb & Intersecting Orbital Chrome Sculpture** with specular lighting bands, floating synthetic satellites, and optical crosshairs that morphs its material and geometry across `Chrome`, `Holographic`, and `Retro Tech` variants.
- **Region C — Functional Hardware Console & Live Variant Bar:** Integrated millennium control deck housing the primary chrome CTA, live `$ npx @kennedys0/taste-engine init --style y2k-futurism` command bar with tactile copy button, and instant expression switcher (`Chrome` / `Holographic` / `Retro Tech`).
- **No Generic Stat Cards or Boast Badges:** Do not place 4 generic KPI boxes beneath the headline or clutter the opening viewport with self-congratulatory engineering badges.

## Style-specific quality gate

Before marking a Y2K Futurism page or component complete, verify every gate:

1. **Gate 1 (Four Authentic Materials):** Are Liquid Chrome (multi-band horizon reflection), Iridescent Spectral accents, Translucent Colored Plastic, and Synthetic Metallic Hardware Panels visibly distinct and authentically rendered?
2. **Gate 2 (Asymmetric Hero & Centerpiece):** Does the hero feature an asymmetric layout with a recognizable Y2K sculptural centerpiece rather than plain centered text over 4 stat boxes?
3. **Gate 3 (Three-Tier Typography):** Does display type use expressive futuristic styling (chrome fill, outline-and-fill, wide tracking) while interface labels and body copy remain effortlessly legible?
4. **Gate 4 (Deep Variant Differentiation):** Does switching between `Chrome`, `Holographic`, and `Retro Tech` transform material finishes, centerpiece geometry, and panel styling rather than just changing an accent color?
5. **Gate 5 (Contrast & Dual-Theme Integrity):** Do both Light Mode (Pearl Silver metallic environment) and Dark Mode (Deep Graphite & Liquid Chrome) maintain ≥ 4.5:1 WCAG AA contrast with zero text collisions?
6. **Gate 6 (Balanced Card Stacks & 320px Mobile Safety):** Are all multi-column card grids and split-column card stacks (`flex: 1 1 0%`) flush and balanced at top and bottom with zero empty voids or `3+1` orphan control wraps, and does the layout adapt cleanly down to `320px` width with `prefers-reduced-motion` support?

## Universal anti-slop gate (self-contained)

Every element must earn its place. **Content before decoration. Do not decorate uncertainty.** Do not invent balances, metrics, testimonials, users, logos, approval, verified/live/online status, fake activity, or success confirmations; use honest empty/loading/error states instead. Demo data is acceptable only if requested and visibly labeled.

Do not add decorative **card-inside-card** layers: a nested surface must represent a distinct interaction or information boundary. Avoid auto-generated 3-feature grids, 4-stat dashboards, fake hero claims, purposeless charts, badge/chip/icon spam, unrelated decorative art, and random loops merely to fill the viewport. Prefer grouping with typography, spacing, alignment and dividers. Equal peers need intentional visual balance.

**Anti-slop copywriting & zero emojis:** no generic "Unlock your potential", "Experience the future", "Supercharge your workflow", "seamless" claims, or translated equivalents. Headings must communicate actual content, CTAs must name the action, and empty/error/success text must describe real states and next steps. Avoid fake urgency, made-up counts, vague AI hype, and inaccessible all-caps walls. Emojis are strictly forbidden as UI icons, button prefixes, or badges across all styles; interface elements must exclusively use clean vector SVGs from Lucide, Radix, Feather, or Heroicons with currentColor inheritance. Adapt the wording to this style and the user's language without changing the truth.

**Theme and mode contrast integrity:** When a user requests dark mode or light mode (or builds a theme switcher), background and foreground colors MUST flip in synchronized pairs across all surfaces, text, borders, inputs, and semantic badges. Never leave black text on dark background, white text on white background, or unreadable low-contrast status pills. All states must meet WCAG AA contrast (minimum 4.5:1 for body/normal text, 3:1 for large headlines and interactive boundaries).

**Responsive 320px baseline and fluid typography:** compose mobile deliberately down to 320px minimum viewport width with zero horizontal blowout. Scale typography down smoothly while respecting readability minimums (body >= 1rem / 16px, secondary >= 0.875rem, code >= 0.8125rem). In top navigation bars, collapse navigation behind an accessible hamburger menu or disclosure with comfortable tap targets on mobile (< 768px). Circular geometry must enforce aspect-ratio: 1 / 1; flex-shrink: 0;. Static by default; use motion for meaningful interactions, respect reduced-motion preferences, avoid bounce/floating/pulsing decoration without a real state.

**Critical/High findings block completion:** fabricated status/data, broken actions, inaccessible/clipped controls, misleading copy, theme contrast collisions or significant mobile failure must be fixed. Report what was actually verified; do not claim tests that did not run.

**Not an aesthetics veto:** expressive colors, shadows, gradients, card layouts or decorative treatments are legitimate when they are intentional parts of the selected aesthetic and pass usability checks. Do not silently force minimalism.

## Anti-slop: style-specific

- **FAIL:** A conventional dark SaaS landing page with centered headline text, 4 generic telemetry stat cards, and thin grey card borders.
- **FAIL:** Unbalanced split-column card stacks where a tall card sits next to shorter stacked cards leaving dead empty space below, or `3+1` orphan-wrapped buttons/checkboxes.
- **FAIL:** Self-congratulatory quality badges (*"WCAG AA 4.5:1"*, *"Zero AI-Slop"*, *"New 10th Style"*) dominating the hero viewport.
- **FAIL:** Flat 2-stop grey gradients pretending to be chrome without a sharp horizon reflection band.
- **FAIL:** Rainbow text gradients (`-webkit-background-clip: text`) applied to multi-line body copy or form labels.
- **FAIL:** Unreadable wide display fonts used for body paragraphs.
- **FAIL:** Emojis used as interface icons, bullets, or status indicators.
- **PASS:** Multi-band liquid chrome reflections, molded translucent colored plastics, controlled iridescent accents, an asymmetric hero with a sculptural metallic centerpiece, 3-tier futuristic typography, and deep differentiation across `Chrome`, `Holographic`, and `Retro Tech`.

## Optional Anime.js v4 recipe

For projects that justify external coordinated motion, use Anime.js v4 with lifecycle cleanup and reduced-motion compliance:

```javascript
import { animate } from 'animejs';

// Respect user motion preferences
const prefersReduced = window.matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches;

export function animateY2KPress(element) {
  if (prefersReduced) return;
  
  animate(element, {
    translateY: [0, 1.5, 0],
    duration: 120,
    ease: 'outQuad',
  });
}
```

## Quality tiers (standalone)

**FLOOR (must pass):** correct and truthful data/state, working critical actions, accessible focus/labels/contrast, usable responsive UI and reduced-motion support. **REFLEX (explain or fix):** generic feature grids, purposeless nested cards, decorative labels/icons, default marketing hype or constant motion. A reflex is allowed when it clearly expresses the chosen style and serves the task. **FINISH:** refinement of alignment, typography rhythm and edge cases. Never treat the selected style itself as a violation. Do not claim browser testing unless actually performed.
