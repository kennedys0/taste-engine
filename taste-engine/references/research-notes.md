## Why the differences matter

Taste Engine's central promise is user-owned taste. A fixed universal ban on glassmorphism would contradict an explicit Glassmorphism selection. Likewise, mandatory animation contradicts low-motion taste and accessibility. Some external sample-writing advice advocates realistic-sounding fabricated numbers; this violates Taste Engine's hard truth floor. No external default outranks explicit user preference and real content.
