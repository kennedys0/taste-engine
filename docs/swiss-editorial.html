[ 04 / DESIGN RATIONALE ]

Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifications on physical affordance, typographic scale contrast, zero drop shadows, and dark mode coupling.

Why does Swiss Editorial strictly forbid rounded corners and drop shadows?
International Typographic Style (born in Zürich and Basel in the 1950s) treats the surface as ink on paper. Rounded corners and decorative shadows introduce arbitrary softness that conflicts with mathematical grid discipline. Spatial depth is communicated purely through typographic scale, weight, and 1px structural dividing rules.
How does dramatic typographic scale contrast work without mobile bloat?
We employ strict fluid typography via CSS `clamp()`: display headlines scale smoothly from 56px–72px on large desktop down to 32px–40px on mobile viewports. Tight letter-spacing tracking (`-0.035em`) prevents word-wrapping blowout, maintaining authoritative tension down to 320px screens.
How does the Theme Coupling rule ensure instant readability?
In Swiss Editorial, dark and light modes represent an authoritative polarity inversion. In light mode, paper is pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and ink is deep black (`#111111`) delivering an 18.5:1 contrast ratio. In dark mode, canvas flips to obsidian (`#0D0D0D`) and typography flips to bone white (`#F5F5F5`). Primary buttons invert cleanly (black block with white text flips to white block with black text).
Can I install just the Swiss Editorial skill standalone in my agent?
Yes! Run `npx skills add kennedys0/taste-engine --skill taste-style-swiss-editorial` to install only this standalone package without pulling other styles.
