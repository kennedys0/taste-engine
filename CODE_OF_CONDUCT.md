# Code of Conduct

Taste Engine contributors agree to participate constructively and respectfully. Disagreement over visual choices is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, and unsolicited private contact are not.

When reviewing a contribution, discuss the work and its evidence, not the contributor. Be clear about trade-offs and acknowledge that accessibility, correctness, and user consent outrank personal aesthetic preferences.

To report conduct problems, use GitHub's private security reporting option if enabled, or contact the repository owner privately through their published GitHub profile. Do not publish personal information or sensitive allegations in public issues.

Maintainers may remove abusive content or restrict participation when needed.
